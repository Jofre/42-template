    * the quotient column only         -> the division itself, or its sign
    * the remainder column only        -> the sign C gives a remainder; it
                                          follows the dividend, not the divisor
    * only negative operands           -> C truncates toward zero, it does not
                                          floor
    * both columns, every line         -> the results are written to the wrong
                                          places, or not through the pointers
