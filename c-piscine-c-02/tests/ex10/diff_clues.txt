  Two things are printed: the destination and the return.
    * the return column alone diverges -> what the subject says is returned; it
                                          is a property of the SOURCE, not of
                                          what was copied
    * size 0 diverges                  -> whether anything may be written at all
    * the destination is one short     -> where the terminator goes when the
                                          source does not fit
    * truncated cases lose their terminator -> the guarantee that separates this
                                          from strncpy
