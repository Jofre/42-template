    * arrays with EQUAL neighbours -> whether equal counts as sorted; the
                                 comparator's sign is what decides, and the

    * a descent the comparator calls out of order -> the corpus holds no
                                 array in the reverse of f's order (whether
                                 one counts as sorted is ex04_reverse_output's
                                 question, at strict), so a descent here is
                                 out of order under every reading: which pair
                                 did your function not ask f about?
    * only the last pair wrong -> the loop bound: how many pairs of neighbours
                                 does an array of n elements have, and does
                                 your loop reach every one of them?
