  Which FAMILY of inputs diverges, not which single number?
    * only 0 disagrees                 -> zero is neither of the two answers you
                                          are choosing between; the subject says
                                          which one it counts as
    * every negative, or every positive -> the comparison is the wrong way round
    * everything, one character         -> the character written, or a missing
                                          newline, rather than the decision
    * only INT_MIN                      -> negating before comparing; INT_MIN has
                                          no positive counterpart
