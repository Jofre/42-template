  A string, start and len; the substring is printed as hex: decode it to read
  it as text. Every start here is at most the string's length.
    * start on the terminator   -> what is left of s to take from there?
    * len past what is left     -> is len how many bytes you copy, or how
                                   many you may copy at most?
    * len of SIZE_MAX           -> what does a sum such as start + len, or
                                   len + 1, become when len is that big?
    * len near UINT_MAX         -> is len, or a count made from it, ever kept
                                   in a type narrower than size_t?
    * one byte short, or one too many -> where does the substring end, and
                                   where does its terminator go?
    * only strings holding a byte of 0x80 or more -> whether a comparison or a
                                   test in your loop sees that char as negative
    * only the long strings     -> does anything stop working past a few
                                   thousand bytes?
